# About the Philippines and government overview sources

Observed 2026-09-08. The GOV.PH portal is being developed through a community collaboration partnership. Linked primary sources support the factual content recorded below.

## Content

- Philippine Statistics Authority, [2024 Census of Population](https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/population-and-housing/node/1684077791): official population of 112,729,484 as of 2024-07-01 and 18 administrative regions.
- Philippine Statistics Authority, [Philippine Standard Geographic Code summary](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/summary): 18 regions, 82 provinces, 149 cities, 1,493 municipalities, and 42,010 barangays as of 2026-06-30.
- Embassy of the Philippines in The Hague, [General Information](https://thehaguepe.dfa.gov.ph/index.php/2014-03-12-01-20-59/2014-03-12-01-57-04): 7,641 islands, three principal island groups, and approximately 300,000 km² of territory.
- Supreme Court E-Library, [1987 Constitution](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/3/353): democratic and republican State, three branches, constitutional commissions, official languages, and national territory.
- Supreme Court E-Library, [Republic Act No. 8491](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/4626): national flag, anthem, coat of arms, and Great Seal.
- Supreme Court E-Library, [Proclamation No. 615](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/56755): Philippine eagle as the national bird.
- National Commission for Culture and the Arts, [Philippine National Symbols](https://ncca.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/BALANGHAY-MAY-JUNE-2012.pdf): Proclamation No. 652 designating the narra and sampaguita as national tree and flower.
