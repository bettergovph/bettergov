> **Who this is for:** Buyers and sellers of titled land, or of a house and lot, held as a capital asset (not by a real-estate dealer) who need a new Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) in the buyer's name.
>
> **What it costs:**
>
> - Capital gains tax (CGT): 6% ([NIRC Sec. 24(D)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html); still 6% after CMEPA per [RR 21-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2021-2025%20Digest.pdf)).
>
> - Documentary stamp tax (DST): 1.5%, or ₱15 per ₱1,000 ([NIRC Sec. 196](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
>
> - Transfer tax: up to 0.5% in provinces, or up to 0.75% in cities and Metro Manila municipalities ([RA 7160 Secs. 135 and 151](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
>
> - eCAR: ₱100 certification fee + ₱30 DST ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
>
> - Registry of Deeds registration fees ([LRA Citizen's Charter 2024](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LRA_CC_03302024-1st-Edition.pdf)).
>
> **How long it takes:**
>
> - BIR eCAR: about 7 working days after a complete filing ([RMC 28-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2028-2025.pdf)).
>
> - Registry of Deeds new title: 19 working days, 2 hours and 35 minutes ([LRA Citizen's Charter 2024](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LRA_CC_03302024-1st-Edition.pdf)).
>
> - Plus time for the notary and the LGU treasurer.

## How the taxes are computed

| Tax | Rate and base | Deadline | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CGT (BIR Form 1706). By law, a tax on the seller. | 6% of the gross selling price or fair market value, whichever is higher | Within 30 days from notarization of the deed | [NIRC Secs. 24(D), 51(C)(2)(b)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html); [RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf) |
| DST (BIR Form 2000-OT) | ₱15 per ₱1,000 (1.5%) of the consideration or fair market value, whichever is higher | Within 5 days after the end of the month the deed was notarized | [NIRC Sec. 196](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html); [RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf) |
| Local transfer tax | Up to 0.5% (provinces) or 0.75% (cities and Metro Manila municipalities) of the consideration or fair market value, whichever is higher | Within 60 days from execution of the deed | [RA 7160 Secs. 135, 151](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html) |

- **Fair market value:** BIR zonal values remain in force until the new Schedule of Market Values (SMV) under the Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act takes over. That Act also amends the tax bases in NIRC Sec. 24(D) and LGC Sec. 135(a) ([RA 12001 Secs. 31, 38](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_12001_2024.html)).
- **Selling your principal residence:** It may be exempt from CGT if you use the proceeds to buy or build a new principal residence within 18 months ([NIRC Sec. 24(D)(2)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)).
- **Ordinary assets** (property used in business, or held by dealers) do not pay CGT. They are subject to creditable withholding tax, filed on BIR Form 1606 by the 10th of the following month ([RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf)).
## Before you start
- [ ] Make sure both parties have TINs. A one-time taxpayer can get one with BIR Form 1904 ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
- [ ] Get a certified true copy of the title from the Registry of Deeds ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- [ ] Get a certified true copy of the latest tax declaration from the Assessor ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- [ ] Pay all real property tax and get a realty tax clearance ([LRA Circular 10-2020](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/LRA_Circular_No_10-2020_-_Official_List_of_Documents_required_for_Registration_of_Sale_of_Registerd_and_Unregistered_Lands.pdf)).
- [ ] Keep the owner's duplicate copy of the title safe. The Registry of Deeds needs it ([LRA Circular 10-2020](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/LRA_Circular_No_10-2020_-_Official_List_of_Documents_required_for_Registration_of_Sale_of_Registerd_and_Unregistered_Lands.pdf)).
- [ ] Agree in writing who will pay which tax and fee.
## Steps
1. Sign the Deed of Absolute Sale (DOAS) and have it notarized. The notarization date starts the BIR deadlines ([RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf)).
2. File and pay the CGT (Form 1706) within 30 days of notarization ([RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf)).
3. File and pay the DST (Form 2000-OT) within 5 days after the end of the notarization month ([RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf)).
4. Submit the ONETT documents to the BIR RDO where the property is located. These include the notarized DOAS, the certified title, the certified tax declaration, the TIN verification and proof of tax payments ([RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf); [BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
5. Claim the electronic Certificate Authorizing Registration (eCAR). BIR will also stamp every page of the deed. An eCAR from the eCAR System stays valid until it is presented to the Registry of Deeds; no revalidation is needed ([RR 12-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2012-2024.pdf); [LRA Circular 10-2020](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/LRA_Circular_No_10-2020_-_Official_List_of_Documents_required_for_Registration_of_Sale_of_Registerd_and_Unregistered_Lands.pdf)).
6. Pay the transfer tax at the provincial or city treasurer within 60 days of the deed ([RA 7160 Sec. 135](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
7. File at the Registry of Deeds. Bring the following ([LRA Circular 10-2020](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/LRA_Circular_No_10-2020_-_Official_List_of_Documents_required_for_Registration_of_Sale_of_Registerd_and_Unregistered_Lands.pdf)):
	- The BIR-stamped deed.
	- The eCAR.
	- The owner's duplicate title.
	- The certified tax declaration.
	- The realty tax clearance.
	- The transfer tax receipt.
8. Pay the Registry of Deeds fees ([LRA Citizen's Charter 2024](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LRA_CC_03302024-1st-Edition.pdf)):
	- ₱30 entry fee.
	- Registration fee, based on value.
	- ₱344.93 IT fee per deed.
	- ₱60 per owner's duplicate title, plus a ₱215.58 IT fee per title.
	- ₱30 per annotation, including the eCAR annotation.
	- Legal research fee: 1% of the registration fee, minimum ₱10.
9. Claim the new TCT in the buyer's name.
10. Bring the new title to the city or municipal Assessor to transfer the tax declaration to the new owner. Ask the Assessor for the local document checklist.
## Deadlines and penalties
- **Late CGT, DST or 1606:** A 25% surcharge applies ([NIRC Sec. 248](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)), plus interest at 12% a year, which is double the legal rate ([TRAIN Sec. 249](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
- **Late transfer tax:** Surcharge and interest follow the local revenue code, within the caps in [RA 7160 Sec. 168](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html) (up to 25%, plus up to 2% a month for at most 36 months).
- **eCAR processing:**
	- One-time transaction case: 3 working days (simple) or 7 (complex) ([RMC 28-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2028-2025.pdf)).
	- eCAR issuance: 7 working days ([RMC 28-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2028-2025.pdf)).
- **Filing method:** Returns are filed electronically, or manually if the electronic platforms are unavailable ([RMC 75-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf)).
