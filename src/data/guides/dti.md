> **Who this is for:** Filipinos aged 18 or older who will run a business alone under a business name. Foreign nationals, recognised refugees and stateless persons must also go to a DTI office with extra documents ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq); [BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).
>
> **What it costs:** ₱200 (barangay), ₱500 (city/municipality), ₱1,000 (regional) or ₱2,000 (national), plus ₱30 documentary stamp tax ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
>
> **How long it takes:** Online. Your certificate is emailed once payment goes through ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)). You must pay within 7 calendar days of applying ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).

## Before you start
- [ ] Pick your territorial scope: barangay, city/municipality, region or national. It sets your fee and where you may operate under that name ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
- [ ] Prepare a business name with a "dominant name" plus a "descriptor" for what you do, for example "Liwanag Bakery" ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).
- [ ] Avoid words like "Corporation", "Company" or "Incorporated". These are for SEC-registered entities ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
- [ ] Have a valid ID and an email address you can access. Your certificate and verification codes are sent there ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).
- [ ] Get an e-wallet, card or Landbank [Link.Biz](http://Link.Biz) ready for payment ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).
## Steps
1. Go to [bnrs.dti.gov.ph](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph), choose **New Registration** and click "I Agree" ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).
2. Fill out the owner's information and confirm it.
3. Select your territorial scope and enter your dominant name and descriptor.
4. Click **Check Name Availability**. If the name is taken, try another.
5. Write down your Reference Code. You will need it for every later transaction.
6. Fill out your business address, personal information, residence and other details.
7. Review the summary and confirm it, then agree to the Undertaking.
8. Pay the fee within 7 calendar days using DTI Teller, GCash, Maya, Landbank [Link.Biz](http://Link.Biz) or a card. If you do not pay in time, the application is deemed abandoned and nullified ([BNRS Registration Guide](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/resources/registration-guide)).
9. Download your Certificate of Business Name Registration. You can also get it later through **Transaction Inquiry** with your Reference Code and an email verification code ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
## What happens next
- **The DTI certificate is not a business permit.** You still need a Mayor's Permit from your city or municipality ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)). See the Mayor's Permit guide.
- **Register with BIR.** Do this on or before you start doing business, meaning your first sale or 30 days after your DTI registration or Mayor's Permit, whichever comes first ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
- **Validity:** 5 years from registration ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
- **Renewal windows** ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)):
	- Early: up to 180 days before expiry.
	- Regular: within 90 days after expiry.
	- Grace: a further 90 days, with a 50% surcharge on the registration fee.
- **Ownership cannot be transferred.** A new owner must register a new business name ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Typo in your registration:** Only typographical errors in new registrations can be corrected. Email the helpdesk with your name, business name, the correction and a government ID ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
- **Moving outside your scope:** If you move or expand beyond your territorial scope, change your scope. This is treated as a new application ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
- **Applying through someone else:** A representative may file with a valid ID and an authorization letter from the owner ([DTI BNRS FAQ](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)).
- **BNRS Helpdesk** ([BNRS](https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq)):
