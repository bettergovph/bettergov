> **Who this is for:** Every Filipino citizen is automatically covered by the National Health Insurance Program under the Universal Health Care Act (RA 11223). You register to get a PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and to pay contributions if you are a direct contributor. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html)
>
> **What it costs:** Registration is free. Direct contributors pay 5% of monthly basic income, effective January 2025. The minimum is ₱500 a month, for incomes up to ₱10,000, and the maximum is ₱5,000 a month, for incomes of ₱100,000 and up. [\[2\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf) [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html) Employees pay half and employers pay half. [\[4\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/members/formal/registration.php)
>
> **How long it takes:** Online PIN applications are processed in 3 to 5 working days, and the PIN is sent by email. [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)

## Member types
- **Direct contributors** pay premiums themselves or with their employer. They include employees, self-earning people, professionals, OFWs, migrant workers, Filipinos with dual citizenship, and lifetime members. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html)
- **Indirect contributors** have premiums paid by the government. They include indigents, 4Ps beneficiaries, senior citizens, PWDs, Sangguniang Kabataan officials and others under the law. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html)
## Before you start
- [ ] Download and fill in the PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF). [\[6\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/downloads/membership/pmrf_012020.pdf)
- [ ] Prepare your PSA birth certificate or a valid ID. Online applications need a scan. [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)
- [ ] List your dependents with their birth or marriage certificates, if you are declaring any. [\[6\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/downloads/membership/pmrf_012020.pdf)
- [ ] Use an email address that has not been used for another PIN application. Only one application is allowed per email. [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)
## Steps: online
1. Open the [PhilHealth PIN application page](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml). [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)
2. Enter your details as they appear on your PMRF. [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)
3. Upload your birth certificate or valid ID. [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)
4. Submit, and wait 3 to 5 working days for your PIN by email. [\[5\]](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/pinApplication.xhtml)
## Steps: employees
1. Give two completed PMRF copies to your employer's HR. [\[4\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/members/formal/registration.php)
2. Check that your employer reports you and deducts your half of the premium from your pay. [\[4\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/members/formal/registration.php)
## Steps: self-earning, voluntary or informal-sector members
1. Bring two PMRF copies and your supporting document to a Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) or PhilHealth Express. [\[3\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/members/informal/registration.php)
2. Receive your PIN and Member Data Record (MDR). [\[3\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/members/informal/registration.php)
3. Pay your first premium monthly, quarterly, semi-annually or annually through PhilHealth, accredited collecting agents, the Member Portal or the eGov PH app. [\[2\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf)
4. Pay on time: the deadline is the last working day of the month or quarter you are paying for. [\[3\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/members/informal/registration.php)
## What happens next
- **Eligibility is immediate.** You do not need a PhilHealth ID card to use benefits. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html)
- **Missed contributions:** direct contributors pay them with interest. The law sets at least 3% per month for employers, and up to 1.5% per month for self-earning members. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html)
- **Example contributions:** ₱10,000 income → ₱500; ₱30,000 → ₱1,500; ₱100,000 → ₱5,000 a month. [\[2\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf)
- **Rate history:** the 5% rate was set for 2024–2025 in RA 11223. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html) In 2023, the scheduled increase was suspended and the rate stayed at 4%, with an ₱80,000 income ceiling. [\[7\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/ofclstmnts/2023/os2023-01-04.pdf) The 5% rate took effect in January 2025, and PhilHealth's July 2026 advisory still applies it. [\[2\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Duplicate PINs:** do not register twice. Ask an LHIO to merge duplicate records.
- **Employer not remitting:** check your contributions in the Member Portal, then report it to PhilHealth. [\[2\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf)
**Help:** PhilHealth Action Center (02) 8662-2588, [actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph](mailto:actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph). [\[2\]](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf)
