> **Who this is for:** Filipino citizens applying for a first passport, renewing, replacing a lost or damaged one, or applying for a child under 18. Only Filipino citizens can get a Philippine passport. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
>
> **What it costs:** ₱950 regular processing or ₱1,200 expedited, the same for new and renewal. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/) Payment centres add a ₱50 convenience fee. [\[8\]](https://passport.gov.ph/faqs_2) Lost, damaged or mutilated passports add a ₱350 penalty. [\[3\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-renewal-epassport/) Booking the appointment is free. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
>
> **How long it takes:** Regular: 10 working days in Metro Manila (NCR), 12 working days outside NCR. Expedited: 5 working days in NCR, 7 outside NCR. Abroad: about 4 to 6 weeks. These dates are tentative and do not include courier delivery. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)

## Before you start
- [ ] Book a confirmed online appointment at [passport.gov.ph](https://passport.gov.ph/). Appointments made anywhere else are not legitimate. Courtesy Lane applicants (seniors, PWDs, pregnant women, solo parents, OFWs, children 7 and below) may walk in. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/) [\[7\]](https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11983_2024.html)
- [ ] Print the application form and e-receipt generated after booking and payment. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- [ ] **New adult applicants:** bring your PSA birth certificate on security paper (original and photocopy). [\[2\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-new/)
- [ ] **New adult applicants:** bring one acceptable valid ID with one photocopy, e.g. PhilID/ePhilID, UMID, SSS card, LTO driver's licence, PRC ID, voter's ID or certificate, postal ID. Pag-IBIG, TIN and PhilHealth IDs are not accepted. [\[5\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/valid-ids-for-passport-application/) [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- [ ] **Renewing:** bring your current ePassport with a photocopy of the data page. [\[3\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-renewal-epassport/)
- [ ] **Married women using the spouse's surname:** bring your PSA marriage certificate. You do not need it if you keep your maiden name. [\[2\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-new/)
- [ ] **Minors (under 18):** bring the child's PSA birth certificate, a valid ID for the child and for the accompanying adult, and a notarised Special Power of Attorney (SPA) if a parent will not appear. If the parents are not married, the mother accompanies the child or signs the SPA. [\[4\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/minor-new/)
- [ ] **Lost passport:** bring a police report in English and a notarised affidavit of loss. You pay a ₱350 penalty. [\[3\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-renewal-epassport/)
- [ ] **Late-registered birth certificate:** bring an extra primary ID, or two of: NBI clearance, school records, government service record, or PhilHealth Member Data Record. [\[2\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-new/)
You do not need to bring a passport photo. DFA captures your photo and biometrics on site. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
## Steps
1. Go to [passport.gov.ph](https://passport.gov.ph/) and choose **Schedule an Appointment**. [\[6\]](https://passport.gov.ph/)
2. Pick the DFA consular office or Temporary Off-site Passport Services (TOPS) site, date and time. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
3. Fill in the online form exactly as your PSA documents read. Major errors can lead to rejection. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
4. Pay the processing fee (₱950 regular or ₱1,200 expedited) through an accredited payment channel. Fees are non-refundable and non-transferable. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/) [\[6\]](https://passport.gov.ph/)
5. Print the confirmation, application form and receipt from your email. If no email arrives within 24 hours of paying, call (02) 8234-3488. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
6. Arrive on your appointment date with your originals and photocopies.
7. Have your photo, fingerprints and signature captured at the consular office. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
8. Keep your official receipt. It shows the tentative release date. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
9. Claim the passport at the same office where you applied, or wait for courier delivery if you chose it. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
10. Check every detail on the data page before you leave. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
## What happens next
- **Validity:** 10 years if you were 18 or older when you applied, 5 years if you were under 18. This comes from Section 12 of RA 11983 (New Philippine Passport Act, 2024), which repealed RA 8239 as amended (including RA 10928). The DFA FAQ refers to this rule as "Section 10". [\[7\]](https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11983_2024.html) [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Someone else claiming:** an immediate family member needs an authorisation letter. Anyone else needs a Special Power of Attorney (SPA). Both must bring the IDs, the DFA receipt and, for renewals, the old passport. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Unclaimed passports** are cancelled one year after the release date. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Renewal:** you may renew at any time. DFA suggests renewing within one year of expiry. There is no penalty for renewing an expired passport. Most countries need at least six months of validity for travel. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Old visas** stay valid in your old passport. Carry both passports. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Do not buy plane tickets** until you have the passport in hand. [\[6\]](https://passport.gov.ph/)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **No slots showing:** refresh the booking page later, because slots open from time to time. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Missed appointment:** you can reschedule only for exceptional or emergency reasons, within 6 months of the original date, by emailing the consular office. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Error printed in the passport:** reapply and pay ₱950. [\[1\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/passport-faq/)
- **Name or date mismatch in documents:** fix the PSA record first (RA 9048/10172 annotation) before applying. [\[2\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-new/)
- **Fixers:** using them is illegal and carries penalties under RA 11983. [\[7\]](https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11983_2024.html)
