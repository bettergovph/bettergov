> **Who this is for:** People forming a stock corporation (2 to 15 incorporators), a One Person Corporation (OPC), a non-stock corporation, or a partnership with the Securities and Exchange Commission ([RA 11232 Sec. 10](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html); [SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
>
> **What it costs:** The filing fee for the Articles of Incorporation is 1/5 of 1% of authorized capital stock, or of the subscription price if higher, with a minimum of ₱2,000. Add a 1% legal research fee (minimum ₱10), the by-laws fee and other small fees ([SEC MC 3 s.2017](https://appointment.sec.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/2017MCno03-new2.pdf); [SEC Registration Fee Calculator](https://www.sec.gov.ph/online-services/registration-calculator/)). No minimum capital is required unless a special law says otherwise ([RA 11232 Secs. 12 and 117](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html)).
>
> **How long it takes:** Under Regular Processing, SEC emails the review result within 7 working days ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).

## Before you start
- [ ] Decide the type: a regular stock corporation (2 to 15 incorporators) or an OPC (a single stockholder who is a natural person, trust or estate) ([RA 11232 Secs. 10 and 116](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html)).
- [ ] Choose a company name. An OPC must add "OPC" to its name ([RA 11232 Sec. 120](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html)).
- [ ] Decide your authorized capital stock and subscriptions. These set your fees ([SEC Registration Fee Calculator](https://www.sec.gov.ph/online-services/registration-calculator/)).
- [ ] For an OPC, name a nominee and an alternate nominee and get their written consent ([RA 11232 Sec. 124](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html)).
- [ ] Choose a processing option on eSPARC: OneSEC, Regular, or ZERO (Zuper Easy Registration Online, which uses digital signing so nothing is sent on paper) ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
## Steps (Regular Processing)
1. Open [esparc.sec.gov.ph](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/) and choose Regular Processing for your company type ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
2. Submit your proposed company name.
3. Enter the Articles of Incorporation and By-laws details.
4. Wait for SEC's email on the review status, sent within 7 working days. If SEC sends a compliance notice, correct the data and resubmit ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
5. Pay the fees on the Payment Assessment Form through eSPAYSEC or Landbank over the counter ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
6. Receive your Certificate of Incorporation.
7. Send two hard copies of the signed and notarized documents with your proof of payment to SEC by courier or registered mail. Do this within 60 calendar days of the approval date on the certificate ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
8. Get your corporate TIN and SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG employer numbers through the Philippine Business Hub, which eSPARC links to ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
## What happens next
- **Business permit and BIR:** Get your Mayor's Permit, then register with BIR on or before starting operations. That means your first sale or 30 days after SEC registration, whichever comes first ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
- **OPC officers:** Appoint a treasurer and corporate secretary within 15 days of incorporation, and notify SEC within 5 days of their appointment ([RA 11232 Sec. 122](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html)). Check with SEC for the required appointment forms and submission instructions.
- **Single stockholder as treasurer:** Check with SEC about the bond and filing requirements if the single stockholder will also serve as treasurer.
- **Annual reports:** File the General Information Sheet and audited financial statements through SEC eFAST ([SEC eFAST list of reportorial requirements](https://efast.sec.gov.ph/user/static/media/sec_list_of_reportorial_requirements_08042025.15eccfaa.pdf)).
- **Term:** A corporation exists perpetually unless its articles say otherwise ([RA 11232 Sec. 11](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11232_2019.html)).

## Common problems and where to get help
- **Application disapproved:** Edit the fields named in the compliance notice and resubmit, or cancel and re-apply ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
- **Hard-copy submission deadline:** If you cannot meet the 60-day deadline, contact the SEC registration office for guidance.
- **OPC representative:** For BIR registration, an OPC representative needs a Written Resolution; a Special Power of Attorney is not accepted ([RMC 74-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2074-2025.pdf)).
- **SEC registration help:** [cprd_registration@sec.gov.ph](mailto:cprd_registration@sec.gov.ph) ([SEC eSPARC](https://esparc.sec.gov.ph/)).
