> **Before you renew:** Check [LTO announcements](https://www.lto.gov.ph/) for changes to renewal schedules, validity periods and applicable penalties.

> **Who this is for:** Holders of a Philippine driver's licence that is about to expire or expired no more than 2 years ago (plain renewal). If yours expired more than 2 years ago, you must also take the exams. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
>
> **What it costs:** ₱585 licence fee. Late penalties: +₱75 if expired 1 day to 1 year (₱660 total); +₱150 if over 1 to 2 years (₱735); +₱225 plus a ₱100 application fee if over 2 years (₱910). [\[1\]](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/13-CC2024-DL-FEE.pdf) The medical exam at an LTO-accredited clinic is paid separately. Ask the clinic for its fee before your appointment.
>
> **How long it takes:** About 35 minutes at the LTO office for plain renewal, per the 2025 Citizen's Charter. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)

## 5 or 10 years?
Under RA 10930 (2017), licences are valid for 5 years, expiring on your birthday. On renewal, you get a **10-year** licence only if you had no violations of the Land Transportation and Traffic Code or other traffic laws during the 5-year period. Otherwise you get another 5-year licence. [\[3\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10930_2017.html) Under the LTO demerit-point system, 5 to 9 demerit points mean you must take the Driver's Reorientation Course. 10 to 39 points mean you also take a theoretical exam. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
## Before you start
- [ ] Settle all unpaid traffic violations. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
- [ ] Get a medical exam at an LTO-accredited clinic, which uploads your medical certificate electronically. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
- [ ] **First renewal of a 5-year licence:** complete the Comprehensive Driver's Education (CDE) online on the LTO portal. You do not need it if your student permit was issued on or after 3 August 2020, or if you already renewed on or after 28 October 2021. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
- [ ] Bring your current driver's licence. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
- [ ] **Leaving the country:** you may renew early, within 1 year before expiry, if you show your plane ticket. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
## Steps
1. Log in to the [LTMS portal](https://portal.lto.gov.ph/) and start a licence renewal application. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
2. Choose your LTO office and appointment date. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
3. Pay ₱585, plus any penalty, online or at the office. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf) [\[1\]](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/13-CC2024-DL-FEE.pdf)
4. Go to the LTO office on your appointment date with your licence. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
5. Have your photo, signature and fingerprints captured. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
6. Claim your new licence card. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
## What happens next
- **Collecting your licence:** Ask your LTO office when your new licence card will be ready for collection.

- **Lost licence:** apply for a duplicate (₱355 licence fee). [\[1\]](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/13-CC2024-DL-FEE.pdf)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Expired more than 2 years:** you must renew with written and practical exams, and the higher penalty applies. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf) [\[1\]](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/13-CC2024-DL-FEE.pdf)
- **Medical certificate not showing in LTMS:** ask the clinic to re-upload it before your appointment. [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
**Help:** Email [clientcare@lto.gov.ph](mailto:clientcare@lto.gov.ph). [\[2\]](https://ltoeasternvisayas.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025-lto-citizens-charter.pdf)
