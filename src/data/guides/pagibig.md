> **Who this is for:** Coverage is mandatory for employees covered by SSS or GSIS, self-employed people, OFWs, uniformed personnel, kasambahays and other earning groups. People aged 18 to 65 can join voluntarily, including non-working spouses. [\[1\]](https://mpm.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/HDMF-Circular-No.-460-Pag-ibig-HDMF-Table-2024.pdf) [\[2\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2009/ra_9679_2009.html)
>
> **What it costs:** Registration is free. Monthly savings are 2% of your monthly fund salary from you and 2% from your employer, or 1% from you if you earn ₱1,500 or less. The fund salary is capped at ₱10,000 since February 2024, so the most required is ₱200 from you and ₱200 from your employer. You may save more. Self-employed members pay both shares. [\[1\]](https://mpm.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/HDMF-Circular-No.-460-Pag-ibig-HDMF-Table-2024.pdf)
>
> **Registration:** Register online for a permanent Membership ID (MID) number. Contact Pag-IBIG for help with your application status. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)

## What the MID number is
The Pag-IBIG Membership ID (MID) number is your permanent Pag-IBIG account number. You use it for remitting contributions, creating a Virtual Pag-IBIG account, and applying for loans. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
## Before you start
- [ ] Prepare a working mobile number for the one-time PIN (OTP) and an email address. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
- [ ] Get your passport or two valid primary IDs, and be ready to take a selfie for account verification. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
- [ ] Check that your employer details (employer ID) are available, if you are employed.
## Steps
1. Go to [Pag-IBIG online membership registration](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/OnlineMembershipRegistration). [\[4\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/OnlineMembershipRegistration)
2. Fill in your personal, address and employment details, then submit to get a Registration Tracking Number (RTN). [\[4\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/OnlineMembershipRegistration)
3. Get your permanent MID number. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
4. Create a Virtual Pag-IBIG account with your MID, name, birth date and mobile number, then verify with the OTP. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
5. Upload your ID and selfie to complete verification. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
6. Give your MID to your employer so it can remit your contributions, or pay your own contributions if you are self-employed or a voluntary member. [\[1\]](https://mpm.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/HDMF-Circular-No.-460-Pag-ibig-HDMF-Table-2024.pdf)
## What happens next
- **Membership term:** your savings mature after 20 years, with 240 monthly contributions. [\[2\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2009/ra_9679_2009.html)
- **Employer share:** your employer must match its share and may not deduct it from your wages. [\[1\]](https://mpm.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/HDMF-Circular-No.-460-Pag-ibig-HDMF-Table-2024.pdf) [\[2\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2009/ra_9679_2009.html)
- **Housing loan:** Before applying, confirm your eligibility, contribution requirements and loan terms with Pag-IBIG.
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Two MID numbers:** ask Pag-IBIG to merge your records. Do not register again.
- **Contributions not posted:** check your records in Virtual Pag-IBIG, then ask your employer for proof of remittance. [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx)
**Help:** Email [contactus@pagibigfund.gov.ph](mailto:contactus@pagibigfund.gov.ph). [\[3\]](https://www.pagibigfundservices.com/virtualpagibig/FAQs.aspx) Hotline (02) 8724-4244.
