> **Who this is for:** SSS members retiring or with a disability, and the beneficiaries of a member who has died.
>
> **Retirement:** You qualify at 60 if you are separated from work or have stopped self-employment, or at 65 regardless. For a monthly pension, you need at least 120 monthly contributions. With fewer, you get a lump sum. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11199_2019.html)
>
> **Death or disability:** These become a monthly pension if at least 36 monthly contributions were paid before the semester of the death or disability. Otherwise they are paid as a lump sum. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11199_2019.html)
>
> **What it costs:** Filing is free.
>
> **Application and follow-up:** File retirement claims through My.SSS. For death or disability claims, contact an SSS branch about the application and expected processing time. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/) [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/death-benefit/)

## How much you get
- **Minimum monthly pension:** ₱1,200 with at least 10 credited years of service, or ₱2,400 with at least 20 credited years. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11199_2019.html)
- **Dependent's pension:** 10% of your monthly pension or ₱250, whichever is higher, for each minor child, up to 5 children. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11199_2019.html)
- **13th-month pension:** paid every December. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
- **Lump-sum option:** retirees may take the first 18 months as a discounted lump sum. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11199_2019.html)
- **Pension increases:** the Pension Reform Program raises pensions every September from 2025 to 2027. The second increase was moved up to June 2026: +10% for retirement and disability pensioners, +5% for death and survivor pensioners. [\[6\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/69th-SSS-Anniversary-Supplement.pdf)
## Before you start
- [ ] Register for My.SSS at [member.sss.gov.ph](https://member.sss.gov.ph/members/). [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
- [ ] Set up a disbursement account, such as a MySSS Card, UMID-ATM, or bank or e-wallet account approved through the Disbursement Account Enrollment Module (DAEM). The MySSS Card automatically becomes your default account. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/) [\[7\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
- [ ] **Retiring at 60 to 64:** get a certificate of separation from your employer, or an affidavit that you have stopped self-employment. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
- [ ] **Disability:** get the SSS Medical Certificate completed by your doctor (issued within 6 months), plus your medical records. [\[3\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/disability-benefit/)
- [ ] **Death:** get the member's PSA death certificate, your PSA marriage certificate (spouse) and the children's PSA birth certificates. [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/death-benefit/)
- [ ] **Representative filing:** get a Letter of Authority or SPA dated within 6 months (Philippines) or 1 year (abroad). [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
## Steps: retirement (online)
1. Log in to My.SSS. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
2. Apply for retirement benefits through My.SSS. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
3. Choose your disbursement account and pension option (monthly, or 18-month advance plus monthly). [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
4. Upload any required documents, then submit. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
5. Watch your email and My.SSS for the claim status. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
## Steps: disability
1. File the disability claim at any SSS branch within 10 years of the disability. Bring the SSS Medical Certificate, completed by your doctor within 6 months of filing. [\[3\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/disability-benefit/)
2. Attend the evaluation or physical examination by an SSS medical specialist, if scheduled. [\[3\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/disability-benefit/)
3. Submit the Annual Confirmation of Pensioners (ACOP) when required, to keep your pension active. [\[3\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/disability-benefit/)
## Steps: death and survivor pension
1. Identify the beneficiaries. Primary beneficiaries are the dependent spouse and dependent children under 21. If there are none, the secondary beneficiaries are the dependent parents. [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/death-benefit/)
2. File over the counter at any SSS branch. A qualified dependent legal spouse with an SS number and a My.SSS account may file online, under Circular 2022-009. [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/death-benefit/)
3. Submit the death, marriage and birth certificates and IDs. [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/death-benefit/)
## What happens next
- Pensions are credited monthly to your disbursement account. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
- Disability pensioners who fail to appear for the yearly Annual Confirmation of Pensioners (ACOP) examination have their pension suspended. The pension is cancelled if they return to work or recover. [\[3\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/disability-benefit/)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **No disbursement account on file:** enrol one in My.SSS (DAEM) before filing. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
- **Retirement claims that must be filed at a branch:** these include members with outstanding SILP, privatization, educational or vocational loans, members with dependent children under guardianship, incapacitated members, members covered by portability law or bilateral agreements, and claims for re-adjudication. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/retirement-benefit/)
**Help:** SSS hotline 1455. Email [usssaptayo@sss.gov.ph](mailto:usssaptayo@sss.gov.ph). [\[5\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/contact-us/)
