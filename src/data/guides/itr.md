> **Who this is for:** Employees who earn only salary (purely compensation income) and do *not* qualify for substituted filing. The most common case is having two or more employers in the same year ([RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf)).
>
> **What it costs:** Filing is free. You pay any income tax still due under the graduated rates ([RA 10963 (TRAIN)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
>
> **Filing deadline:** The deadline is **15 April** each year ([NIRC Sec. 51(C)(1)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)). For tax year 2025, BIR moved it to **15 May 2026** ([RMC 30-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2030-2026.pdf)).

## Do you need to file at all?
You do **not** need to file Form 1700 if any of these apply ([RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf)):
- Your taxable compensation income is ₱250,000 or less.
- You had only one employer for the whole year and it withheld your tax correctly. This is "substituted filing": your employer's BIR Form 2316 stands in for your return.
- All of your income was already subject to final tax.
- You are a minimum wage earner.
- You are an overseas Filipino worker (OFW) for your income earned abroad.
You **must** file if you worked for two or more employers during the year, at the same time or one after another. RR 4-2024 says such earners "are not qualified for substituted filing" ([RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf)).
**What is BIR Form 2316?** It is the Certificate of Compensation Payment/Tax Withheld that your employer gives you. It shows what you earned and how much tax was withheld. Under substituted filing, it serves as your return. If you file Form 1700, you need one Form 2316 from each employer.
## Before you start
- [ ] Get a BIR Form 2316 from every employer you had during the year.
- [ ] Have your TIN and RDO code ready.
- [ ] Download the latest Offline eBIRForms package (v7.9.6.0 as of April 2026) from the BIR website ([RMC 36-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2036-2026.pdf)). Or log in to eFPS if you are enrolled ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
- [ ] Check you have a working email address. BIR sends your filing confirmation there ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
## Steps
1. Open eBIRForms and select **1700v2018** ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
2. Enter your background information (TIN, RDO, name, address).
3. Enter the compensation and tax withheld from each Form 2316.
4. Check the computed tax due against the graduated rates below.
5. Validate the form, then click Submit/Final Copy to file online ([RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf) requires electronic filing unless the platforms are unavailable).
6. Screenshot the email confirmation pop-up and keep the confirmation email. These are your proof of filing; a "Received" stamp is not required ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
7. Pay any balance by the deadline. Use an ePay channel such as LANDBANK Link.BizPortal, UnionBank, or DBP PayTax/MyEG/Maya, or pay over the counter at an Authorized Agent Bank ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)). Revenue Collection Officers accept cash only up to ₱20,000 ([RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf)).
8. Submit any required attachments through the eAFS portal within 15 days after the deadline ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
If you need help, BIR eLounges prioritise employees with two or more employers and people filing for loans or travel ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
## Income tax rates (2023 onwards)
Source: [RA 10963 (TRAIN), Sec. 24(A)(2)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html).

| Taxable income per year | Tax |
| --- | --- |
| ₱250,000 or less | 0% |
| Over ₱250,000 to ₱400,000 | 15% of the excess over ₱250,000 |
| Over ₱400,000 to ₱800,000 | ₱22,500 + 20% of the excess over ₱400,000 |
| Over ₱800,000 to ₱2,000,000 | ₱102,500 + 25% of the excess over ₱800,000 |
| Over ₱2,000,000 to ₱8,000,000 | ₱402,500 + 30% of the excess over ₱2,000,000 |
| Over ₱8,000,000 | ₱2,202,500 + 35% of the excess over ₱8,000,000 |

## Deadlines and penalties
- **Deadline:** 15 April of the following year ([NIRC Sec. 51(C)(1)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)). BIR sometimes extends it. The 2025 returns were moved to 15 May 2026 ([RMC 30-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2030-2026.pdf)). The return for tax year 2026 falls due 15 April 2027 unless BIR announces otherwise.
- **Where to file:** Electronically, or at any Authorized Agent Bank, RDO or authorized software provider. You are no longer penalised for filing at the "wrong" RDO ([RA 11976 (EOPT)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html); [RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf)).
- **Late filing or payment:** A 25% surcharge applies ([NIRC Sec. 248](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)). Interest is charged at double the legal interest rate a year ([TRAIN Sec. 249](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)), which works out to 12%. Compromise penalties may also apply.

## Common problems and where to get help
- **Two employers, but both withheld tax:** You must still file Form 1700, because the combined income can push you into a higher bracket ([RR 4-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%204-%202024.pdf)).
- **No confirmation email:** Do not refile right away. Check your spam folder, then contact BIR.
- **You also have business or freelance income:** Use Form 1701 (mixed income) instead of Form 1700.
- **BIR Contact Center:** (02) 8538-3200 and [contact_us@bir.gov.ph](mailto:contact_us@bir.gov.ph) ([BIR Contact Us](https://www.bir.gov.ph/contactus)).
