> **Who this is for:** Self-employed people, freelancers, professionals and sole proprietors whose income comes *purely* from business or practice of a profession, **and** who either (a) use the graduated rates with the 40% Optional Standard Deduction (OSD), or (b) chose the 8% flat rate ([RMC 17-2019](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2017-2019.pdf)). If you also have a salary, or you itemize deductions, use Form 1701 instead.
>
> **What it costs:** Filing is free. Under the 8% option, the tax is 8% of gross sales or receipts and other non-operating income above ₱250,000. It replaces both the graduated income tax and the 3% percentage tax ([TRAIN Sec. 24(A)(2)(b)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html); [RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
>
> **Filing deadline:** The annual deadline is **15 April** ([NIRC Sec. 51(C)(1)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)). It was extended to **15 May 2026** for tax year 2025 ([RMC 30-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2030-2026.pdf)).

## Which rate are you on?
- **8% flat rate:** Only available if your gross sales/receipts plus other non-operating income do not exceed the ₱3,000,000 VAT threshold, and you are not VAT-registered ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- **Choosing it:** You must elect the 8% rate in your **first-quarter** income or percentage tax return, or in the first quarterly return after you start your business. Otherwise you are placed on graduated rates. The choice cannot be changed for that year ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- **If you pass ₱3M during the year:** You automatically move to graduated rates for the whole year. Your 8% quarterly payments are credited ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- **Graduated rates + OSD:** You deduct a flat 40% of gross sales/receipts instead of listing expenses ([TRAIN Sec. 34(L)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)). You then pay graduated income tax plus 3% percentage tax if you are non-VAT ([TRAIN Sec. 116](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
- **Micro and small taxpayers** (gross sales below ₱3M is "micro") may instead use the simplified Form 1701-MS. It was added to eBIRForms v7.9.6.0 ([RR 8-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%208-%202024.pdf); [RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf); [RMC 36-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2036-2026.pdf)).
## Before you start
- [ ] Confirm your Certificate of Registration lists Form 1701/1701A (or 1701-MS) and your chosen rate ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
- [ ] Total your gross sales/receipts for the year from your registered books and invoices.
- [ ] Gather your quarterly returns (1701Q) and proof of payment for the year.
- [ ] Collect BIR Form 2307 certificates from clients who withheld tax from your payments.
- [ ] Install the latest Offline eBIRForms package (v7.9.6.0 as of April 2026) ([RMC 36-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2036-2026.pdf)), or log in to eFPS if you are enrolled.
## Steps (eBIRForms walkthrough)
1. Open Offline eBIRForms and choose **1701A** ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
2. Fill in your TIN, RDO code, name and business details.
3. Tick the tax regime you elected in the first quarter: "8%" or "graduated rates with OSD".
4. Enter your total gross sales/receipts and any other non-operating income.
5. Enter your quarterly payments and the creditable tax withheld shown on your 2307s.
6. Validate the return and correct any errors flagged.
7. Submit the return online and wait for the BIR confirmation email ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
8. Pay any balance through an ePay channel (LANDBANK Link.BizPortal, UnionBank, DBP PayTax/MyEG/Maya) or at an Authorized Agent Bank ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)).
9. Upload the required attachments (for example 2307s) through eAFS within 15 days after the deadline ([RMC 20-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026.pdf)). Under the 8% option, financial statements are not required ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)). Under OSD, they are not required either ([TRAIN Sec. 34(L)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
## Deadlines and penalties

| Return | Due | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1701Q (Q1, Q2, Q3) | 15 May, 15 August, 15 November | [TRAIN Sec. 74](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html) |
| 1701A (annual) | 15 April of the following year (2025 returns: 15 May 2026) | [NIRC Sec. 51](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html); [RMC 30-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2030-2026.pdf) |
| 2551Q percentage tax (graduated-rate, non-VAT filers only) | Within 25 days after the end of each quarter | [EOPT Sec. 128](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html) |

- **Late filing or payment:** 25% surcharge ([NIRC Sec. 248](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)), plus interest at double the legal rate, which is 12% a year ([TRAIN Sec. 249](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
- **Micro and small taxpayers** get lower penalties ([RR 6-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%206-%202024.pdf); [EOPT Sec. 45](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html)):
	- 10% surcharge.
	- Interest at 50% of the regular rate (6%).
	- ₱500 per failure to file an information return, capped at ₱12,500 a year.
	- Compromise penalties cut by 50%.
## Common problems and where to get help
- **I forgot to elect 8% in Q1.** You are on graduated rates for the whole year, and the choice cannot be changed until next year ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- **I also have a job.** Use Form 1701 (mixed income), not 1701A ([RMC 17-2019](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2017-2019.pdf)).
- **I became VAT-registered.** The 8% option is no longer available ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- **BIR Contact Center:** (02) 8538-3200 and [contact_us@bir.gov.ph](mailto:contact_us@bir.gov.ph) ([BIR Contact Us](https://www.bir.gov.ph/contactus)).
