> **Who this is for:** Couples marrying in the Philippines, in a civil or religious ceremony. Both of you must be at least 18. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
>
> **What it costs:** Each city or municipality sets its own fees. For example, Legazpi City charges ₱300 for the application, ₱200 for the licence, and ₱100 for family planning. [\[4\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf) Indigent couples are exempt from licence fees under the Family Code. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html) PSA copies of supporting documents cost extra; see the CENOMAR and birth certificate guides.
>
> **How long it takes:** At least 10 consecutive days, the required posting period for the application, before the licence can be issued. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html) Once issued, the licence is valid anywhere in the Philippines for 120 days. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)

## Before you start
- [ ] Find the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) of the city or municipality where either of you usually lives. You apply there. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
- [ ] Get PSA birth certificates for both partners. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html) [\[4\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf)
- [ ] Get PSA CENOMARs for both partners, if your LCR requires them. Legazpi does, for example. [\[4\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf)
- [ ] Prepare valid IDs for both partners. [\[4\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf)
- [ ] **Ages 18 to 20:** get written parental consent. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
- [ ] **Ages 21 to 24:** ask for parental advice. If advice is refused or not obtained, the licence is delayed by 3 months. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
- [ ] **Previously married:** get the death certificate of your late spouse, or the court decree of annulment or nullity. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
- [ ] **Foreign partner:** get a certificate of legal capacity to marry from their embassy or consulate. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
- [ ] **Religious wedding:** ask your church about its own requirements, such as Pre-Cana seminars, baptismal and confirmation certificates. These are church rules, not civil law.
## Steps
1. Attend the family planning and responsible parenthood session at your local health or family planning office, and get the Certificate of Compliance. RA 10354 says this certificate is required before the licence is issued and is free of charge. [\[2\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10354_2012.html)
2. If either of you needed parental consent or advice, complete marriage counselling and get the certificate. Without it, the licence is delayed by 3 months. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
3. Appear together at the LCR to file the marriage licence application under oath. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html) [\[4\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf)
4. Pay the LGU fees. [\[4\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf)
5. Wait for the 10-day posting period to end. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
6. Claim the marriage licence. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
7. Hold the ceremony within 120 days. You need an authorised solemnising officer: a judge, priest, rabbi, imam or minister of a registered church, a ship captain or military commander in specific cases, a consul abroad, or a city or municipal mayor. You also need at least two witnesses aged 18 or over. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html) [\[3\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)
8. Sign the marriage certificate with your officer and witnesses. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
9. Confirm that the solemnising officer sends the marriage certificate to the LCR within 15 days of the ceremony. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
10. Ask the Local Civil Registrar when your marriage record will be forwarded to PSA, then request your PSA marriage certificate once the record is available.
## When you do not need a licence
The Family Code exempts some cases, including marriage in danger of death, and a man and woman who have lived together as husband and wife for at least 5 years without any legal impediment to marry. They must sign an affidavit instead. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
## What happens next
- A valid PSA marriage certificate stays valid as long as it is intact and readable (RA 11909). [\[5\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11909_2022.html)
- A wife may keep her maiden name or use her husband's surname. If she uses his surname on her passport, she must show the PSA marriage certificate. [\[6\]](https://consular.dfa.gov.ph/adult-new/)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Licence expired before the wedding:** after 120 days the licence lapses, and you must apply again. [\[1\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1987/eo_209_1987.html)
- **Marriage not yet on PSA records:** ask the LCR where the marriage was registered to endorse it to PSA.
- **Fees and schedules differ:** check your city or municipal Citizen's Charter, which LGUs must publish under RA 11032.
**Help:** Your city or municipal Civil Registrar. For PSA document issues, use the contacts in the CENOMAR guide.
