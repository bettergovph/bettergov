> **Who this is for:** BIR-registered businesses and professionals who must register for VAT, plus anyone who chooses to register voluntarily. You must register if:
>
> - Your gross sales for the **past 12 months** exceeded ₱3,000,000, or
>
> - There are reasonable grounds to believe they will exceed ₱3,000,000 in the **next 12 months**.
>
> Sources: [EOPT Sec. 236(F)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html); [RR 3-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.3-%202024.pdf).
>
> **What it costs:** BIR charges no registration fee. The ₱500 Annual Registration Fee ended on 22 Jan 2024 ([RMC 14-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2014-2024.pdf)). Once registered, you charge 12% VAT on your sales ([RR 3-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.3-%202024.pdf)).
>
> **Processing time:** Ask your RDO for the expected processing time when you submit your registration update.

## Before you start
- [ ] Add up your gross sales for the last 12 months, and estimate the next 12, excluding VAT-exempt sales ([EOPT Sec. 236(F)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html)).
- [ ] Download the October 2025 version of BIR Form 1905 (registration update) ([RMC 97-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2025.pdf)).
- [ ] Check the document checklist for your type of business ([RMC 74-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2074-2025.pdf)).
- [ ] Know that the 8% income tax option ends once you register for VAT ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- [ ] If registering voluntarily, plan to stay VAT-registered for at least 3 years ([EOPT Sec. 236(G)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html)).
## Steps
1. Fill out BIR Form 1905 to add "Value-Added Tax" as a tax type ([RMC 97-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2025.pdf)).
2. File it with your RDO, electronically (ORUS) or in person ([EOPT Sec. 236](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html)).
3. Get your updated Certificate of Registration showing VAT. Pay ₱30 DST if a new COR is issued ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
4. Apply for new invoices (Authority to Print, or a permit for loose-leaf or computerized invoices) that show VAT details ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)). Each VAT invoice must show:
	- The words "VAT Reg TIN" followed by your TIN and branch code.
	- The VAT amount as a separate item.
5. Stop using your old non-VAT invoices.
6. Charge 12% VAT on every sale and issue an invoice for every sale, whatever the amount ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
7. Record your sales and purchases separately so you can claim input VAT.
8. File BIR Form 2550Q (April 2024 version) and pay within **25 days after the end of each quarter** ([TRAIN/EOPT Sec. 114](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html); [RMC 68-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2068-2024.pdf)).
## What changes for your invoices and books
- **Invoices replace official receipts.** Under EOPT, the "Invoice" is now the main sales document for both goods and services. An Official Receipt is only a supplementary document ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
- **Services are taxed on gross sales.** VAT on services is now based on "gross sales", the same as goods, rather than on cash received ([RR 3-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.3-%202024.pdf)).
- **No more monthly 2550M.** VAT returns are quarterly only, starting 1 January 2023. This change came from the TRAIN law, not EOPT ([RA 10963 Sec. 114](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
- **Threshold will be updated.** The ₱3M threshold is adjusted for inflation every 3 years ([EOPT Sec. 109(CC)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html); [RR 3-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.3-%202024.pdf)). Check current BIR issuances when determining whether you must register.
## Deadlines and penalties
- **If you should have registered but didn't:** You must pay VAT as if registered, but you cannot claim input tax credits for that period ([EOPT Sec. 236(F)(2)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html)).
- **Registration deadline:** Contact your RDO as soon as your sales reach the registration threshold to confirm when you must file your registration update.
- **Late filing or payment:**
	- 25% surcharge ([NIRC Sec. 248](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)).
	- 12% a year interest ([TRAIN Sec. 249](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html)).
	- Micro and small taxpayers pay a reduced 10% surcharge and 6% interest instead ([RR 6-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%206-%202024.pdf)).
- **Cancelling VAT registration:** Possible if you expect sales over the next 12 months to stay at or below the threshold, or if you close the business. The cancellation takes effect on the first day of the following month ([EOPT Sec. 236(E)(2)](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html)).
## Common problems and where to get help
- **"My old receipts say Official Receipt."** The transition period ended on 31 December 2024. Issuing an "Official Receipt" for sales after 30 June 2024 is penalised ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
- **"I'm on 8% and crossed ₱3M."** You are moved to graduated rates for the year and must register for VAT ([RR 8-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%208-2018.pdf)).
- **BIR Contact Center:** (02) 8538-3200 and [contact_us@bir.gov.ph](mailto:contact_us@bir.gov.ph) ([BIR Contact Us](https://www.bir.gov.ph/contactus)).
