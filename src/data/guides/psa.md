> **Who this is for:** Anyone who needs a PSA-issued copy of a birth certificate for school, work, a passport, benefits or court. You can request your own, your child's or your parent's, or one on behalf of the owner with authorisation. [\[3\]](https://psahelpline.ph/faq) [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
>
> **What it costs:** ₱155 per copy at a PSA Civil Registry System (CRS) outlet. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/) Online with home delivery: ₱330 through PSA Serbilis, or ₱365 through PSAHelpline. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate) [\[2\]](https://www.psahelpline.ph/) A view-only online copy is ₱130. [\[5\]](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)
>
> **How long it takes:** At an outlet, about 1 hour if the record is in the database, or 7 working days if it is not. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/) Delivery in the Philippines takes about 3 to 9 working days through Serbilis, or the next working day in Metro Manila and 3 to 8 working days elsewhere through PSAHelpline. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate) [\[2\]](https://www.psahelpline.ph/)

## Before you start
- [ ] Write down the exact details as registered: full name, date and place of birth, and both parents' full names (mother's maiden name). [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
- [ ] Prepare a valid ID for whoever will receive the document. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate) [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
- [ ] **Requesting for someone else at an outlet:** bring the owner's ID, your own ID and an authorisation letter or SPA. A representative may request for at most 2 unrelated people. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
- [ ] Choose your channel: walk-in with appointment, PSA Serbilis delivery, PSAHelpline delivery, or view-only online copy.
## Steps: walk-in at a PSA CRS outlet
1. Book a free appointment at [crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/). Minors cannot book. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
2. Go to the outlet on your date with your appointment slip and ID. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
3. Fill in the request form. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
4. Pay ₱155 per copy. Some outlets accept GCash or Maya. Outlets in Caloocan, Muntinlupa, Pasig and Valenzuela add a local government fee. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
5. Collect the certificate on security paper on the release date shown on your receipt. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
## Steps: order online for delivery
1. Go to [PSA Serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/) or [PSAHelpline](https://www.psahelpline.ph/). [\[5\]](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/) [\[2\]](https://www.psahelpline.ph/)
2. Choose **Birth Certificate** and enter the details exactly as registered. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
3. Enter the delivery address and the name of the person who will receive it. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
4. Pay online or through the listed payment centres, and keep the reference number. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
5. Receive the document at the address and show a valid ID to the courier. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
## What happens next
- **Validity:** under RA 11909 (Permanent Validity of the Certificates of Live Birth, Death and Marriage Act, 2022), PSA birth certificates stay valid as long as they are intact and readable. Government offices may not require a newer copy. [\[6\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11909_2022.html)
- **Fee exemptions:** Ask your PSA outlet about eligibility and the documents needed to request a fee exemption under RA 11909. [\[6\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11909_2022.html)
- **No record found:** PSA issues a Negative Certification. Your birth may need late registration at your Local Civil Registry Office. Manual searches add about 10 to 15 working days for delivery orders. [\[7\]](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/Delivery)
- **Errors in the record:** a document with notes in the margin can take about 5 working days at ₱255 through the premium annotation service. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Courier could not deliver:** check that the authorised receiver is home and has an ID. [\[1\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
- **Fixers outside outlets:** only use official channels. Report fixers to the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA). [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
**Help:** ARTA hotline 1-ARTA (12782), [complaints@arta.gov.ph](mailto:complaints@arta.gov.ph). Contact Center ng Bayan 0908-881-6565. 8888 Citizens' Complaint Hotline. [\[4\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
