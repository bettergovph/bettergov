> **Who this is for:** Owners, and anyone with beneficial use, of land, buildings or machinery in the Philippines.
>
> **What it costs:** Your LGU sets the rate within these legal caps ([RA 7160 Secs. 233, 235](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)):
>
> - Basic real property tax: up to 1% of assessed value in provinces, or up to 2% in cities and Metro Manila municipalities.
>
> - Special Education Fund levy: 1% on top.
>
> **How long it takes:** Minutes online if your LGU has an e-payment portal. Quezon City sends the official receipt in 2 to 3 business days ([QC how-to](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/qcitizen-guides/how-to-pay-your-real-property-tax/)).

## When to pay
- **When it becomes due:** The tax for the year accrues on 1 January ([RA 7160 Sec. 246](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
- **Paying in instalments:** Pay in four quarterly instalments, due by **31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December** ([RA 7160 Sec. 250](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
- **Early-payment discount:** Your sanggunian may grant a discount of up to **20%** for advance or prompt payment ([RA 7160 Sec. 251](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)). Example: Quezon City gave the following discounts for 2026 RPT ([QC RPT schedule](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/payment-of-real-property-tax-schedule/), 15 Jan 2026):
	- 20% if paid in full by 31 Dec 2025.
	- 10% if paid in full from 1 Jan to 31 Mar 2026.
	- No discount for quarterly payment.
## Before you start
- [ ] Find your Tax Declaration Number (TDN) or Property Index Number on your tax declaration or last receipt ([QC how-to](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/qcitizen-guides/how-to-pay-your-real-property-tax/)).
- [ ] Check your LGU's current rates and discount schedule. They are set by local ordinance.
- [ ] Choose a payment channel: City or Municipal Treasurer, a branch office, or the LGU's online portal.
- [ ] If you recently bought the property, make sure the tax declaration has been moved to your name.
## Steps (worked example: Quezon City online)
1. Log in to [QC E-Services](https://qceservices.quezoncity.gov.ph/) ([QC RPT schedule](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/payment-of-real-property-tax-schedule/)).
2. Choose Real Property Tax and enter your TDN ([QC how-to](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/qcitizen-guides/how-to-pay-your-real-property-tax/)).
3. Review the billing, including any back taxes, and choose to pay annually or quarterly.
4. Pay with GCash, Maya, a credit or debit card, Landbank or UnionBank ([QC RPT schedule](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/payment-of-real-property-tax-schedule/)).
5. Save the payment confirmation, and wait 2 to 3 business days for the official receipt ([QC how-to](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/qcitizen-guides/how-to-pay-your-real-property-tax/)).
6. Keep your receipts. You will need a realty tax clearance when you sell or transfer the property ([LRA Circular 10-2020](https://lra.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/LRA_Circular_No_10-2020_-_Official_List_of_Documents_required_for_Registration_of_Sale_of_Registerd_and_Unregistered_Lands.pdf)).
In other LGUs, go to the City or Municipal Treasurer's Office with your TDN or last receipt, or use the LGU's own e-payment portal.
## Penalties if you miss a payment
- **Interest:** Unpaid RPT earns interest of **2% a month**, for a maximum of **36 months**, so up to 72% of the unpaid amount ([RA 7160 Sec. 255](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)). Interest runs on each missed instalment, not only after a whole year.
- **Collection:** The LGU can levy the property, sell it at public auction, and take other collection action ([RA 7160 Secs. 256 to 258](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
- **Disputing the bill:** Pay the tax "under protest" first, then file a written protest with the treasurer within 30 days of payment ([RA 7160 Sec. 252](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
## Recent changes (RA 12001, Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act)
- **Effective date:** 5 July 2024 ([RA 12001](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_12001_2024.html)).
- **Cap on increases:** In the first year a new Schedule of Market Values (SMV) is used, any RPT increase is capped at 6% ([RA 12001 Sec. 29](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_12001_2024.html)).
- **Amnesty:** Penalties and interest on RPT unpaid before the Act took effect could be waived if paid within 2 years, that is by 5 July 2026 ([RA 12001 Sec. 30](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_12001_2024.html); [BLGF MC 001-2025 (IRR)](https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/BLGF-MC-No.-001.2025-IRR-of-RA-No.-12001-or-the-RPVARA-Reform-Act-6-Jan-2025-Approved-3.pdf)). Ask your local treasurer about any amnesty currently available and its payment deadline.
## Common problems and where to get help
- **"I didn't get a bill."** RPT is still due without a bill. The due dates are set by law ([RA 7160 Sec. 250](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html)).
- **Paid online but no receipt:** In QC, email [rptpayment@quezoncity.gov.ph](mailto:rptpayment@quezoncity.gov.ph) ([QC how-to](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/qcitizen-guides/how-to-pay-your-real-property-tax/)).
- **Quezon City Treasurer's Office** ([QC RPT schedule](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/payment-of-real-property-tax-schedule/)):
	- Phone: 8988-4242 loc. 8317, 8145, 8156 or 8157.
	- Email: [CTO@quezoncity.gov.ph](mailto:CTO@quezoncity.gov.ph).
	- QC Helpline: 122.
- **Elsewhere:** Contact your City or Municipal Treasurer's Office, or the Provincial Treasurer for municipalities outside Metro Manila.
