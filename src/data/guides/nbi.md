> **Who this is for:** Anyone who needs an NBI clearance for work, travel, a visa or another transaction. This covers first-time applicants and renewals. Foreign nationals can apply for the first time only at the NBI Main Office or regional offices. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
>
> **What it costs:** ₱160 total. This is ₱130 for the clearance plus ₱30 e-payment convenience fee. First-time jobseekers with a barangay certificate pay nothing (RA 11261). [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
>
> **How long it takes:** Same visit if you have no "hit" (name match). If you have a hit, the release date is about 8 working days later at the Main Office, or 15 working days at regional, district and satellite clearance centres. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)

## Before you start
- [ ] Prepare an email address and mobile number for the online account at [clearance.nbi.gov.ph](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph). [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
- [ ] Get your original PhilSys National ID, or two other valid IDs. Accepted IDs include passport, driver's licence, UMID, PRC ID, postal ID, voter's ID, PSA birth certificate, or school ID with registration form. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
- [ ] **First-time jobseekers:** get a barangay certificate stating you have lived there for at least 6 months, and sign the oath of undertaking. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf) [\[3\]](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11261_2019.html)
- [ ] **Renewing:** keep your old clearance number. Online renewal is open only to people whose last clearance was issued on or after 16 Oct 2016 and had no hit. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
## Steps
1. Register or log in at [clearance.nbi.gov.ph](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph) and complete your personal details. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
2. Choose **Apply for clearance**, then pick an NBI clearance centre and an appointment date. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
3. Pay ₱160 (₱130 + ₱30) through the e-payment options shown, and keep the reference number. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
4. Go to the clearance centre on your schedule and bring your IDs and reference number. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
5. Have your photo, fingerprints and signature captured. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
6. Wait for your printed clearance, which takes about 15 minutes if there is no hit. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
7. If you have a hit, return on the release date given. You may be asked to appear for a Quality Control interview. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
## Online renewal (no visit needed for biometrics)
1. Log in at [clearance.nbi.gov.ph](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph) and choose renewal using your previous clearance details. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
2. Choose pick-up at the NBI Clearance Building, UN Avenue, Manila (ready after 24 hours), or courier delivery. Delivery costs ₱200 in NCR (about 5 working days) and ₱350 outside NCR (about 7–10 working days). [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
3. Pay the clearance fee and any delivery charge online. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
## What happens next
- **Priority lanes** serve senior citizens, PWDs, pregnant women and frontliners. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
- **Before submitting your clearance:** Check the requirements of the office requesting it, including how recently it must have been issued.
- **A hit does not mean you have a criminal record.** It means your name matches or resembles a name in NBI records and must be checked by hand. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **Paid, but the status is not updating:** keep the payment reference number and email the NBI. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
- **Older fee figures online (₱115, ₱155, ₱165):** these come from older charters or other payment channels. The 2025 Charter lists ₱130 + ₱30. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf) [\[2\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/citizens-charter/)
**Help:** Email [ncic@nbi.gov.ph](mailto:ncic@nbi.gov.ph). Visit the NBI Clearance Building, UN Avenue, Ermita, Manila. For complaints, call the 8888 Citizens' Complaint Hotline or the Civil Service Contact Center ng Bayan (CCB) at 1-6565. [\[1\]](https://nbi.gov.ph/uploads/2025/04/2025-CITIZENS-CHARTER-HANDBOOK-1st-EDITION.pdf)
