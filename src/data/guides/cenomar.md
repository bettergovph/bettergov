> **Who this is for:** People who need proof from PSA records that they have never married. It is usually needed to get a marriage licence, and some embassies or employers ask for it too. [\[5\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf) If you have married before, PSA issues an **Advisory on Marriages** instead, listing the recorded marriages. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
>
> **What it costs:** ₱210 at a PSA CRS outlet. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/) With delivery: ₱430 through PSA Serbilis (includes ₱30 documentary stamp tax), or ₱420 through PSAHelpline. [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate) [\[3\]](https://www.psahelpline.ph/) A view-only online copy is ₱185. [\[6\]](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)
>
> **How long it takes:** 1 working day (8 working hours) at an outlet. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/) Delivery in the Philippines takes about 3 to 9 working days through Serbilis. [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)

## Before you start
- [ ] Check that you are allowed to request it. PSA releases a CENOMAR only to the person named, their authorised representative, spouse, parent, direct descendant, guardian or nearest kin (PSA Memorandum Order 2017-050). [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
- [ ] Know the online limits. Online orders through PSAHelpline are only for yourself, your child or your parents. Other requests must go to an outlet. [\[4\]](https://psahelpline.ph/faq)
- [ ] Prepare the person's full name, date and place of birth, and parents' names as written on the birth certificate. [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
- [ ] Bring a valid ID. A representative at an outlet also needs the owner's ID and an authorisation letter or SPA. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
- [ ] Ask the office that wants the CENOMAR how recent your copy must be.
## Steps: walk-in
1. Book a free appointment at [crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/). [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
2. Go to the outlet with your ID and slip. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
3. Fill in the request form for a Certificate of No Marriage Record. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
4. Pay ₱210 per copy. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
5. Return on the release date, usually the next working day, to collect it. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
## Steps: online with delivery
1. Open [PSA Serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/) or [PSAHelpline](https://www.psahelpline.ph/) and choose **CENOMAR**. [\[6\]](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/) [\[3\]](https://www.psahelpline.ph/)
2. Enter the owner's details and the purpose. [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
3. Pay ₱430 (Serbilis) or ₱420 (PSAHelpline). [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate) [\[3\]](https://www.psahelpline.ph/)
4. Receive the document and show your ID to the courier. [\[2\]](https://crs-itp2.psaserbilis.com.ph/Census/BirthCertificate)
## What happens next: validity
- **Before ordering:** Check how recently your CENOMAR must have been issued with the embassy, employer or office requesting it.
- **For a marriage licence:** the Local Civil Registrar usually asks for both partners' CENOMARs. Legazpi City is one example. [\[5\]](https://legazpi.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/REVISED-CITIZEN-CHARTER-2025-A3.pdf)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **You received an "Advisory on Marriages" but you have never married:** Contact a PSA outlet or your Local Civil Registry Office for assistance with your record.
- **Online order refused because you are not the owner, child or parent:** go to an outlet with an SPA. [\[4\]](https://psahelpline.ph/faq)
**Help:** ARTA 1-ARTA (12782), [complaints@arta.gov.ph](mailto:complaints@arta.gov.ph). Contact Center ng Bayan 0908-881-6565. 8888 hotline. [\[1\]](https://crs-appointment.psahelpline.ph/)
