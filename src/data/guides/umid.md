> **Important change:** SSS no longer issues new generic UMID cards. Under SSS Circular 2025-008, the **MySSS Card** replaced the UMID card and UMID Pay Card. It is a valid government ID and a bank card (EMV debit/ATM) used for SSS benefits. Applications opened on 1 October 2025. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf) [\[3\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/news-and-updates/sss-launches-emv-equipped-dual-function-mysss-card) UMID cards already issued remain valid as IDs. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)

> **Who this is for:** SSS members with a permanent SS number and a My.SSS account who want the MySSS Card (the UMID replacement). [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
>
> **What it costs:** SSS charges nothing to apply, but the partner bank may charge the cost of the card and its own fees. Check with your chosen bank. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf) [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
>
> **How long it takes:** The card arrives within 15 working days in Metro Manila, or 20 working days elsewhere, after the bank opens your account. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)

## Before you start
- [ ] Make sure your SS number is permanent, not temporary. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
- [ ] Register for a My.SSS account at [member.sss.gov.ph](https://member.sss.gov.ph/members/) if you do not have one. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
- [ ] Check that your local address, mobile number and email are updated in SSS records. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
- [ ] Register for the PhilSys National ID. SSS verifies you through National ID eVerify. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf) [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
- [ ] Use a phone with a working camera for the facial scan. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
## Steps
1. Log in to My.SSS and go to **Services → MySSS Card**. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
2. Confirm that your personal details are correct. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
3. Give consent for National ID eVerify. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
4. Complete the live facial scan. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
5. Choose a partner bank. As of September 2026, the active partners are AUB and RCBC. SSS has signed agreements with China Bank, EastWest Rural Bank and UnionBank. [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/69th-SSS-Anniversary-Supplement.pdf)
6. Agree to share your data with the bank and submit. You will get a confirmation. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
7. Finish opening the bank account through the bank's app or a branch, and pay any bank fees. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
8. Wait for the card to be delivered. You do not need to visit an SSS branch for biometrics. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
## What happens next
- The MySSS Card becomes your default account for SSS loans and benefits. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
- You can hold only one MySSS Card at a time. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
- Lost or damaged cards are replaced under the partner bank's rules. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
- Other government agencies are not required to accept the MySSS Card as their own membership card. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
- More than 400,000 MySSS Card accounts had been approved as of 30 July 2026. [\[4\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/69th-SSS-Anniversary-Supplement.pdf)
## Background: what UMID was
The Unified Multi-Purpose ID was created by Executive Order 420 (2005) to unify IDs across government agencies. The implementing rules named SSS, GSIS, Pag-IBIG and PhilHealth as participating agencies. [\[5\]](https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo2005/eo_420_2005.html) The old SSS rules on UMID cards (Circulars 2020-009 and 2021-011) were repealed by Circular 2025-008. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
## Common problems and where to get help
- **The MySSS Card option is missing:** check that your SS number is permanent and your contact details are complete. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
- **National ID verification fails:** make sure your name and date of birth match in SSS and PhilSys records. [\[2\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/mysss-card/)
- **Card delivery or account issues:** contact your chosen partner bank. [\[1\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CI2025-008-External.pdf)
**Help:** SSS hotline 1455. Email [usssaptayo@sss.gov.ph](mailto:usssaptayo@sss.gov.ph). [\[6\]](https://www.sss.gov.ph/contact-us/)
