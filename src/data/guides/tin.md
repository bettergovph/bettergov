> **Who this is for:** First-time employees, self-employed people and professionals, mixed-income earners, and people who need a TIN only for a one-time transaction or a government ID (EO 98).
>
> **What it costs:** Free for employees, one-time and EO 98 applicants. Self-employed and mixed-income registrants pay ₱30 documentary stamp tax (DST) on the Certificate of Registration (COR). The old ₱500 Annual Registration Fee no longer applies. ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf); [RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf); [RMC 14-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2014-2024.pdf))
>
> **How long it takes:** Walk-in, 6 hours (Form 1902 or 1904). Online through your employer on ORUS, 2 days, 7 hours and 55 minutes. Online for business, 3 days. ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf))

## Which form is yours?

| Your situation | BIR form | Deadline to register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee (purely compensation) | 1902 | Within 10 days from the start of employment ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)) |
| Self-employed, professional, or mixed income (job plus business) | 1901 | On or before you start the business. That is your first sale, or 30 days after your Mayor's Permit, PTR or DTI/SEC registration, whichever comes first ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)) |
| One-time transaction (for example, selling property or settling an estate), or a TIN only for a government ID under EO 98 | 1904 | Before payment of the tax (one-time), or whenever you need it (EO 98) ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)) |

The current form versions are the October 2025 editions ([RMC 97-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2025.pdf), 30 Oct 2025). Download them from the BIR website. Older versions may be refused.
## Before you start
- [ ] Check that you have never had a TIN. If you once had one, even from a past job, use it. Do not apply for a second TIN.
- [ ] Prepare one government ID that shows your name, address and date of birth, for example your PhilSys ID or passport ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- [ ] If you are a married woman using your married name, bring your marriage contract ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- [ ] If you are a first-time jobseeker, get a barangay certification. It is listed as an extra document ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- [ ] If you are applying online, take a selfie holding your ID ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- [ ] Self-employed: get your DTI certificate (or SEC/CDA papers) and Mayor's Permit or PTR first, if you already have them. Check the full list in the registration checklist ([RMC 74-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2074-2025.pdf)).
## Steps: new employee
1. Give your new employer your personal details and ID within your first days at work.
2. Ask your employer to apply for your TIN through the Employer Service Link on [ORUS](https://orus.bir.gov.ph). New hires without a TIN are registered by their employer online ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf); [BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
3. Wait for the TIN, which arrives in about 2 days, 7 hours and 55 minutes. There is no fee ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
4. Keep the TIN for life. Employees do not get a Certificate of Registration ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
If your employer cannot apply for you, you may walk in. Fill out Form 1902 and file it at the RDO that covers your employer. Walk-in processing takes 6 hours and is free ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
## Steps: self-employed, professional or mixed income
1. Create an account on [ORUS](https://orus.bir.gov.ph).
2. Choose individual business registration and fill out the Form 1901 details.
3. Upload your ID, your selfie holding the ID, and your business documents.
4. Pay the ₱30 DST for your Certificate of Registration ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf); [BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
5. Wait for your eCOR. Processing takes about 3 days ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
6. Get your invoices authorized (Authority to Print, or a permit for loose-leaf or computerized invoices) ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
7. Register your books of accounts ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
8. Attend the RDO's briefing for new registrants ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
9. Post your COR at your place of business. If you sell online, post it on your page or website ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
Mixed-income earners who already have a TIN from employment do not get a new TIN. File Form 1905 to update their registration instead ([RMC 97-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2025.pdf)).
## Steps: one-time transaction or EO 98 (TIN for an ID)
1. Fill out Form 1904 ([RMC 97-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2025.pdf)).
2. Submit it on ORUS, or at the RDO for your residence. For a one-time transaction, submit it at the RDO that handles the transaction ([RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
3. Receive your TIN. Walk-in takes 6 hours and ORUS takes 3 days. Both are free ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
## What happens next
- **Digital TIN ID:** From 24 April 2026, the BIR Digital TIN ID is available in the eGovPH app. The ORUS Digital TIN ID counts as a valid government ID ([RMC 34-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20NO.%2034-2026.pdf), 28 Apr 2026).
- **Self-employed:** Ask your RDO to confirm the tax returns and filing deadlines that apply to your business after registration.
- **Registration seal badge:** Businesses must display the BIR Registration Seal badge. The deadline was extended to 31 October 2026 ([RMC 99-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2099-2026.pdf), 24 Sep 2026).
- **No yearly fee:** The ₱500 Annual Registration Fee was removed from 22 January 2024. You no longer need Form 0605 for it ([RMC 14-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2014-2024.pdf); [RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf)).
- **Late registration:** Contact your RDO for the applicable requirements and an assessment of any penalties.
## Common problems and where to get help
- **"I already have a TIN from a past job."** Do not apply again. Have it transferred to your new RDO by filing Form 1905 ([RMC 97-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2097-2025.pdf)).
- **"ORUS rejected my ID."** Check that the ID shows your name, address and birth date, and that your selfie is clear ([BIR Citizen's Charter 2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen's%20Charter%202025%202nd%20Edition%20v2.pdf)).
- **BIR Contact Center:** (02) 8538-3200 and [contact_us@bir.gov.ph](mailto:contact_us@bir.gov.ph) ([BIR Contact Us](https://www.bir.gov.ph/contactus)).
